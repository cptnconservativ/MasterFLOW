Method

Steady, uniform open-channel flow by Manning's equation, Q = (k/n)·A·R2/3·S1/2, with k = 1.486 and R = A/P. Critical depth satisfies Q²T/(gA³) = 1 with g = 32.174 ft/s². Froude number Fr = V/√(gD), D = A/T. Unknown depths are solved with Brent's bracketed method (relative depth tolerance 1×10⁻¹², discharge residual ≤ 1×10⁻⁸). Gradually varied, unsteady and pressurised flow are not modelled.
